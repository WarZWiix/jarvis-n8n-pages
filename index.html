JARVIS n8n

JARVIS n8n is a personal, self-hosted automation used by a single person: its owner.

It connects the owner's own Gmail account to a private voice assistant that runs on the owner's own computer. At the owner's request, the assistant can list unread messages and archive, label or delete messages in that same mailbox.

This application is not offered to the public, has no other users, and is not a commercial product.
